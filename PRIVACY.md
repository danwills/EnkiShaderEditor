# Privacy Policy

Enki Shader Editor does not collect, store or share any personal data. It
has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device, and
it contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

## Permissions

Each is asked for only when you use the feature, and you can refuse or
revoke it at any time:
- **Camera:** shows the camera image as a texture in your shaders. Frames
  stay on the device and are never saved unless you capture the output.
- **Microphone:** lets shaders react to sound. Audio is analysed as it plays
  and never recorded or saved.
- **System audio capture:** the same, for audio playing on the device.
  Android shows a notification while it's on.
- **Notifications:** progress for long captures and audio input.
- **Storage** (older Android versions only): saves the images, videos and
  exports you ask for.

Sensors such as the accelerometer, gyroscope and light sensor feed your
shaders while they run. Their readings are never stored or sent anywhere.

## Your files

Your shaders, textures and settings are kept in the app's private storage.
Captures and exports go only where you choose to save or share them.
