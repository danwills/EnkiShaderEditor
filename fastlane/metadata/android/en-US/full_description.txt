Enki Shader Editor is a full GLSL shader workshop that fits in your pocket. Type code and watch it run live behind the editor, then turn sliders, capture stills far beyond screen resolution, record videos, or set your creation as a live wallpaper.

WRITE
• Live preview behind the code, or full screen
• Syntax and error highlighting, with errors shown on the line
• Find in shader, an extra keys row for code, and an FPS display
• Adjust Defines turns your #defines into live sliders and colour pickers

GO BIG
• Multipass shaders with offscreen buffers and a Common section, ShaderToy-style
• Buffers can keep state across frames: simulations, trails, game logic
• Sound passes: shaders that make audio
• Heavy shaders draw progressively, a band at a time, so your phone stays responsive

REACT
• Multi-touch, the keyboard and typed text
• Camera, microphone and the audio playing on your phone
• Accelerometer, gyroscope, light, battery and other sensors
• Your own images as textures

CAPTURE
• Stills at any resolution, with oversampling for clean edges
• Frame sequences, MP4 video and GIFs
• Upscaling filters, CRT looks and colour maps

SHARE
• Use any shader as a live wallpaper
• Install shader packs, and import and export your library

PRIVATE
Enki has no internet permission, no ads and no tracking. Everything you make stays on your device.

Enki is open source (MIT), forked from Markus Fisch's Shader Editor.

Tips: some phones limit the GPU when unplugged, so check performance on battery with the keyboard hidden. For a smooth live wallpaper, aim for at least 30 fps.

Bugs and ideas: https://github.com/danwills/EnkiShaderEditor/issues
