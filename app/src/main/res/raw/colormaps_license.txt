Peter Kovesi, https://colorcet.com

arXiv:1509.03700 [cs.GR] 2015.
The newer maps not in the CSV release (C8-C11, CBD2, CBL3-4, the CBT
tritanopic maps) come from the colorcet package (Holoviz), which carries
them under the same licence.

Ready's colour maps (GollyGang/ready): the ColorBrewer diverging maps
(Cynthia Brewer, Apache License 2.0, as shipped in VTK's vtkColorSeries),
matplotlib's inferno (Nathaniel J. Smith, Stefan van der Walt - CC0), and
Ready's terrain map.
